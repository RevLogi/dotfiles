# Review mode

Test retained understanding from material already marked `developing`, `solid`, or `review`.

1. Select a concept using age, weakness, and relevance; honor an explicit concept argument.
2. Keep the source hidden before the first attempt.
3. Ask one retrieval, prediction, or transfer question at a time and wait for the answer.
4. Re-open the material after the attempt to resolve discrepancies.
5. Record changed evidence and the review date. Downgrade stale or contradicted mastery without penalty language.

## Completion criterion

Complete the initial turn when one question is waiting for the learner. Complete the review after assessing the unaided attempt against the source, recording any changed evidence, and giving one concise assessment plus the next action.
