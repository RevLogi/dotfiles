# Learn mode

Orient the learner without pre-teaching the section.

1. Infer the next useful chunk from `COURSE.md` and `PROGRESS.md`, or use the requested section.
2. Give a small reading target with an exact file, heading, or page location when available.
3. State one objective and two or three things to notice.
4. Ask the learner to return after reading and reconstruct the idea in their own words.

Keep the target section unrevealed until the learner reads it. Advance the recorded position only after the learner confirms completion.

## Completion criterion

Complete the turn when one bounded reading target, one objective, and two or three things to notice are visible, the course position remains accurate, and the learner has one request to return with their reconstruction.
